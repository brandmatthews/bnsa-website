Our work

Projects, from city hall to your street.

Some of our work is slow and procedural. Some of it is a simple conversation with a neighbor. Here's what's underway.

Active

Lighting Ordinance Update

Bend's Outdoor Lighting Standards (BDC 3.5.200) date to 2004. We're working directly with the City to bring shielding requirements, curfews, and color-temperature limits up to current dark sky standards.

Active

Neighbor Lighting Audits

Free evening walkthroughs for residents dealing with light trespass or questions about their own lighting. We help identify the source, suggest low-cost fixes, and provide resources to begin the conversation with a neighbor if needed.

Active

Blue Light Special

Noticed a blue or purple light around town? That is a defective bulb. Volunteers are cataloging these streetlamps and reporting them to the appropriate authorities for replacement.

Active

Bortle Mapping Project

In conjunction with Dark Sky Oregon, volunteers collect sky quality readings across Deschutes County to gather data about sky brightness in the region that we can monitor year over year.

Planned

Dark Sky Certification

Assembling the sky quality data and community support needed to nominate the city of Bend for International Dark Sky Community recognition, joining our neighbors in Sisters are surpassing Flagstaff, AZ as the largest dark sky city on Earth.

Planned

Business Retrofit Program

A proposed rebate program to help local businesses replace unshielded parking-lot and signage lighting with dark sky compliant fixtures.

Have a piece of sky worth protecting?

If you know a site, a stretch of public land, or a neighborhood that could use a lighting audit, we'd like to hear about it.
