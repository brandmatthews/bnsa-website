The sky over Bend is disappearing, one errant light at a time.

We're a volunteer alliance working to protect what's left of Central Oregon's night sky through better lighting policy, neighbor-to-neighbor education, and hands-on restoration projects.

Why it matters

Darkness is a resource, and Bend is running out of it.

Every year, more unshielded lighting spills across our neighborhoods and public lands disrupting sleep, disorienting migrating birds, and erasing a view of the cosmos that shaped this region's identity long before it had a name.

Bend's own outdoor lighting ordinance hasn't been meaningfully updated since 2004. We think it's time it caught up.
