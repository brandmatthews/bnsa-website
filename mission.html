Our mission

Keep Central Oregon's night sky within reach of everything living under it.

The Bend Night Sky Alliance is a volunteer-led organization working to reduce light pollution through policy, education, and direct outreach. Under the DarkSky Oregon umbrella, we aim for a dark sky for everyone.

The problem

Light pollution increases every year in Central Oregon.

Since the city's outdoor lighting ordinance was last written, LED lights have been invented and Bend's population has nearly doubled. The code hasn't been able to keep pace, and neither has awareness of just how much is being lost to sleep, to wildlife, and to the simple experience of looking up and seeing a sky full of stars.

Our approach

Unlike most forms of pollution, light pollution has an off switch.

Three ways we work

  • Policy — Working with city and county officials to push for safe, modernized outdoor lighting ordinances that reflect current science.
  • Education — Helping neighbors and businesses understand and fix light trespass before it becomes an issue.
  • Advocacy — Building the case for dark sky recognition for Bend and joining our neighbors in Sisters and Sunriver as Dark Sky International Certified places.
What we believe

Our values

01

Neighbors first

Most light trespass is unintentional. We want to lead with conversation and shared solutions.

02

Scientific grounding

The increase in light pollution is quantifiable by measurements. The decrease has tangible benefits. Our policy asks are backed by data.

03

Dark skies and safe streets

Good lighting design improves safety and cuts glare at the same time. We don't ask anyone to choose between them.

Where we're headed

A modernized city ordinance, dark sky designation, and a community that can look up and see a starry sky.
