Our mission

Keep Central Oregon's night sky within reach of everyone who lives under it.

Bend Night Sky Alliance is a volunteer-led nonprofit working to reduce light pollution through policy, education, and direct neighbor-to-neighbor outreach.

The problem

Light pollution isn't inevitable. It's a design choice.

Unlike most forms of pollution, light pollution has an off switch. A poorly aimed floodlight, an un-shielded streetlamp, a security light left on till dawn — each one is a small, fixable decision that adds up to a sky-wide loss.

Bend's population has nearly doubled since the city's outdoor lighting ordinance was last written. The code hasn't kept pace, and neither has awareness of just how much is being lost — to sleep, to wildlife, to the simple experience of looking up.

Our approach

Three ways we work

  • Policy — pushing for a modernized outdoor lighting ordinance that reflects current science.
  • Education — helping neighbors understand and fix light trespass before it becomes a conflict.
  • Advocacy — building the case for dark-sky recognition on public land near Bend.
What we believe

Our values

01

Neighbors first

Most light trespass is unintentional. We lead with conversation and shared solutions, not shame or citations.

02

Evidence over opinion

Our policy asks are grounded in current lighting science — not nostalgia, and not guesswork.

03

Dark skies, safe streets

Good lighting design improves safety and cuts glare at the same time. We don't ask anyone to choose between them.

Where we're headed

A modernized city ordinance, a designated dark sky place, and a community that knows how to look up.
